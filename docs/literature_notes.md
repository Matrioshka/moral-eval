ELEPHANT / AITA-NTA-FLIP:
Measures social/moral sycophancy by testing whether models side with the narrator in both original and perspective-flipped AITA posts.

Alexis replication/extension:
Finds reasoning models may show lower measured sycophancy; data fidelity problems in flipped posts inflate the metric; binary labels conflate label-mapping errors, narrator alignment, and genuine user-opinion alignment.

Implication for this project:
This project should not merely measure whether a model changes or sides with a narrator. It should test whether the model distinguishes irrelevant social pressure from relevant moral evidence.

Core construct:
Moral reasoning integrity is selective updating: resisting pressure that does not change the morally relevant facts or arguments, while updating when new facts or stronger reasons genuinely affect the judgement.
